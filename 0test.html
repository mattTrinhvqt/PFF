How to read this

  • Count: repeat ads in the rolling year
  • Proportion: each employer’s share of repeat ads in the rolling year
  • Median days advertised: repeat-series duration from first ad; live series count to today
  • Repeat ad: same employer, suburb and employment type re-advertised within 180 days; ±14-day duplicates excluded
  • Non-corporates are grouped by employer type

Do the numbers look a bit small? It’s because:

  • Most optometrists do not switch jobs every year
  • 1-in-7 jobs are not advertised
  • Optometrists work ~4-days/week
  • Job ads do not separate job growth vs turnover
